# Privacy Policy — Shakers AI Usage (local scan)

## Data collection
Reads, on your machine only: your local AI-tool session logs (Claude `~/.claude/projects`,
Codex `~/.codex/sessions`, Gemini `~/.gemini/tmp`) and your local git history. It never
reads or transmits your source code or file contents.

## On-device redaction
Before anything leaves your machine, the data is reduced to aggregates: counts, activity
metrics, derived tiers, and short secret-scrubbed excerpts. Secrets (tokens, keys,
connection strings) are scrubbed; file paths and message bodies are counted, never sent.

## Usage, storage, sharing
With your explicit consent, the aggregated redacted report is sent to Shakers and attributed
to your verified email, to build your "My work with AI" profile. No third-party sharing
beyond Shakers' own backend.

## Data retention
The redacted report is retained by Shakers per the hosted Shakers privacy policy. You can
request deletion via Shakers support.
